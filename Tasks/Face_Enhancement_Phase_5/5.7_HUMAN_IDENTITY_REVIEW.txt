TASK 5.7 — BLINDED HUMAN IDENTITY AND FABRICATION REVIEW

Objective: assess human-perceived identity preservation and facial-detail fabrication.

Do:
1. Use the Phase 4 blinded review kit where appropriate.
2. Use at least two independent reviewers.
3. Include random samples, targeted degradations, controls and repeats.
4. Blind/randomize treatment where feasible.
5. Assess same-person confidence, identity preservation, identification usefulness, visible fabrication/hallucination, facial-structure alteration and uncertainty.
6. Predefine scoring/adjudication before review.
7. Measure inter-reviewer agreement.
8. Separate visual quality from identity preservation.
9. Report failure examples and degradation-specific patterns.
10. Minimize personal information in review materials.

Acceptance: two or more reviewers complete the planned review with reproducible analysis.
