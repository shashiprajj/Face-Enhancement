TASK R5-4 — FINAL RAPID VALIDATION GATE

Objective
---------
Make the final evidence-based decision about whether A4 is a viable working enhancement candidate.

Review
------
Use:
- R5-1 service evidence
- R5-2 actual Feature-Match comparison
- R5-3 targeted fixes and robustness
- Phase 4 T6/T7 evidence
- CPU runtime/resource observations
- current licensing status

Answer five questions
---------------------
1. Does A4 actually help real low-quality faces?
2. Does enhancement ever turn a correct match into an incorrect match?
3. Does T6/T7 prevent unnecessary enhancement?
4. Does the standalone service reliably produce usable output?
5. Is A4 good enough to continue toward production hardening?

GREEN
-----
Choose GREEN if:
- A4 clearly improves useful real-world Feature-Match outcomes;
- identity errors are absent or acceptably low;
- T6/T7 safely controls enhancement;
- the service behaves reliably;
- CPU behavior is acceptable for the intended next step.

GREEN does NOT mean commercial production approval.
Licensing must still be cleared separately before commercial deployment.

YELLOW
------
Choose YELLOW if:
- A4 helps some cases;
- evidence is encouraging but inconsistent or too small;
- targeted improvement is required;
- licensing or other approval gates remain open.

RED
---
Choose RED if:
- A4 does not improve actual Feature-Match;
- A4 creates unacceptable identity errors;
- routing cannot contain harm;
- the service is unreliable.

If RED:
---------
Do not randomly test more models.

Instead:
1. Extract the real-world failure signatures.
2. Identify which degradation classes A4 fails on.
3. Identify the exact Feature-Match failure mechanism.
4. Use those failures to define the next model-selection benchmark.

Required report
---------------
Keep it concise and decision-oriented:
- final verdict
- number of images tested
- A4 route percentage
- original vs enhanced matching results
- wins/losses/unchanged
- identity flips
- failure/fallback counts
- degradation-specific results
- robustness result
- CPU runtime
- licensing status
- exact next step

Acceptance
----------
A clear GREEN/YELLOW/RED decision exists with reproducible evidence.

Expected effort
---------------
Approximately 1 hour.
