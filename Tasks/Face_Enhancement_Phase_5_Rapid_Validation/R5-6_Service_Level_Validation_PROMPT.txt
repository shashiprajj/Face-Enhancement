R5-5 NAFNet route is intended for strong low-noise blur only.
High-noise, clean/mild, pixelated, halftone, small/unsafe faces, bad detection, bad lighting, and heavy compression should not reach NAFNet.

R5-5 artifact guard checks numeric validity, face validity, Lab chroma, high-frequency color energy, and grayscale preservation.

R5-5 DEV evidence: 47/1,120 eligible; 44 passed; 3 destructive NAFNet outputs; artifact guard caught all 3.

Known limitation: full_image can create a tight face crop with black padding; the quality gate may classify it as NOT_BLUR and return ORIGINAL. This is safe but can reduce coverage, especially for low-resolution archive images.

R5-5 specifically requires R5-6 to compare face_chip and full_image and to run the 200 real photos through full_image.
