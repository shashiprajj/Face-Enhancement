# Phase 4 Completion Plan

## P4-T6 — Do-Not-Enhance Gate
Finish/resume development inference, define measurable quality features, calibrate thresholds on development data only, freeze the gate, then evaluate once on untouched evaluation data.

## P4-T7 — Selective Model Routing
Use the frozen T6 decision plus degradation characteristics to choose original input or an already-benchmarked enhancement candidate. Define safe fallbacks.

## P4-T8 — Evidence Synthesis & Gate
Consolidate SFace, dlib, benchmark, real-world, human-review, licensing and infrastructure evidence. Produce the final Phase 4 gate and Phase 5 scope.

## Non-goals
- No Feature-Match production integration.
- No unconditional enhancement.
- No final production model selection.
- No test-set-driven tuning.
- No treating missing evidence as a pass.
