# Phase 4 Gates

G-1 GPU: target-hardware performance/throughput is unresolved until measured.
G-2 Second evaluator: required for multi-evaluator identity evidence.
G-3 Dataset/legal: every real-world source must have recorded permission/licence and intended-use compatibility.
G-4 Licensing: every candidate/evaluator weight and relevant vendored component requires production-use review.
G-5 Human review: required for fabrication/identity-substitution evidence.

Gate semantics:
GREEN = required evidence complete and supports next decision.
YELLOW = progress/evidence exists but a required production decision remains blocked.
RED = unacceptable identity risk/failure cannot currently be controlled.

Do not infer readiness from model quality alone.
