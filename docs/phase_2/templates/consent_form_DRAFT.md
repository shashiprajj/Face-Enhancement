## What this study is
[Company] is evaluating software that improves the quality of face photographs, for example blurry, dark, or low-resolution photos. We need photos of volunteers taken in good and in poor conditions, so we can measure whether the software keeps the person looking like themselves.

## What we will NOT do
- We will **not** use your photos to recognize, identify, or verify you, or to build a face-recognition system.
- We will **not** store your name, contact details, or ID numbers with the photos. Your photos are labelled with a random code (e.g., `P-3F9A01`).
- We will **not** publish your photos, unless you tick the optional box below.
- We will **not** sell or share your photos outside the named evaluation team.

## What we will collect
- Photos and short video clips of your face, taken by several cameras at different distances, light levels, and movements (about 30–45 minutes).
- Optional, self-reported: age band (e.g., 30–39), gender, skin tone on a 10-point colour scale. Used only for group-level fairness statistics. You may choose "prefer not to say".

## How long and where
- Photos are stored encrypted, on internal systems only, accessible to the named evaluation team.
- Photos are deleted at the end of the project + [90] days, or earlier if you withdraw.
